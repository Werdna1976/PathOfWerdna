# Audio credits

Recorded samples in `samples/`. All are CC0 (public domain): free to use, modify and ship,
with no attribution required. The creators are credited here anyway.

| Folder | Pack | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `samples/kenney_impact/` | Impact Sounds (130 impacts and footsteps) | Kenney (kenney.nl) | CC0 1.0 (see `License.txt`) | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds |
| `samples/kenney_rpg/` | RPG Audio (50 foley sounds) | Kenney (kenney.nl) | CC0 1.0 (see `License.txt`) | https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio |
| `samples/starninjas_sword/` | 20 Sword Sound Effects: attacks | StarNinjas | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes |
| `samples/starninjas_clash/` | 20 Sword Sound Effects: clashes | StarNinjas | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes |

StarNinjas asks for a link to their OpenGameArt profile if you use the sounds:
https://opengameart.org/users/starninjas

Everything else in `sfx/`, `music/` and `ambience/` is generated by `tools/gen_audio.gd`.
How the samples are layered into game sounds is set in `data/audio/recipes.json`.
